## DESIGN decisions and options

**Option 1 — generate numerical forecasts from retrieved/RAG text:** reject. It would make the numeric result hard to reproduce and calibrate, and the current P4-A2 evidence does not establish operational-corpus readiness.

**Option 2 — direct ledger analytics that bypasses the retrieval/RAG dependency:** reject for BUILD. It would silently waive the roadmap's explicit readiness gate and leave the grounded context path unproven.

**Option 3 — selected design direction:** use direct, deterministic, time-aware analytics over authorized canonical ledger history for numeric signals and forecasts. Use governed retrieval/RAG for contextual evidence and citations once the operational retrieval path is proven stable. Preserve the roadmap's P5-A and retrieval/RAG readiness gates for integrated context and whole P5-A2 acceptance. Independently scoped provider-free source/history and numerical analytics BUILD may proceed after its SPEC is accepted and a bounded Work Order is reviewed, using only accepted P5-A A1-A6 evidence and making no operational-RAG, provider, or AI-governance claim. This does not close or waive A7, does not claim integrated context readiness, and does not permit a forecast-driven action.

For each run, preserve the selected as-of cutoff, allowed source IDs/versions/digests, metric and timezone policy, schedule/run identity, algorithm/model version, sample count, result or abstention, confidence/calibration reference, drift/freshness status and approval state. Never mix a forecast with later-created records or count multiple `Report` versions for one shift as independent outcomes. If history is insufficient, timestamps are ambiguous, lineage fails, authorization is stale, retrieval is unavailable, or drift exceeds its approved threshold, show an explicit blocked/stale/abstained result and do not manufacture a forecast.

Signals and forecast outputs may be retained as analytical evidence only under a separately specified immutable receipt policy. Any durable recommendation or operational mutation requires a human decision bound to the exact forecast/run and source digest; stale approvals fail closed. No scheduled task may mutate operational records or take protected action automatically.

Because scheduled runs and forecast/approval outcomes have repeated terminal states, the future SPEC must follow `docs/cvf/INVARIANT_FAMILY_STANDARD.md`: declare a registered family and pin its matrix digest, or record `NOT_APPLICABLE` with a reason. Do not copy a new outcome framework into this artifact or create a matrix during INTAKE/DESIGN.
